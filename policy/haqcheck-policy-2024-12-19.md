## Code of Principles

HaqCheck follows the best practices in fact-checking, which is recognized by the world's leading nonpartisan fact-checking organizations. We adhere to the International Fact-Checking Network's code of principles of commitment to impartiality, transparency, and accuracy.

## How We Rate

At HaqCheck, our fact-checking process follows the CHECKED framework (Claim Selection, Hear (Contact the Claimant), Evidence (Collect Evidence), Consult (Consult Experts), Knowledge (Draft and Review), Edit (Edit and Publish) and Develop (Feedback and Impact)), ensuring that each claim we evaluate is rigorously verified and presented to our audience. The CHECKED process is designed to maintain accuracy, transparency, and accountability in our work. Here's what CHECKED stands for:

**Claim Selection:** we begin our fact-checking process by thoroughly monitoring diverse media sources. We identify potential claims and collectively assess their fact-checkability through a unanimous decision-making process at the Fact-checking Desk. This involves evaluating each claim's verifiability, relevance, and possible public impact. By focusing on these criteria, we ensure that our efforts are dedicated to the most significant and influential claims, aiming to maintain a high standard of public discourse.

**Hear (Contact the Claimant):** Once a claim is selected, HaqCheck reaches out to the individual or organization that made the statement to gather additional context or clarification. This step ensures that the claimant has an opportunity to provide supporting evidence, explain their perspective, or address any ambiguities in their statement. If the claimant is not responsive, we make observations to understand the intent behind the claim based on the text and other available information. Documenting the effort to contact the claimant and any observations made is essential to our process, promoting fairness and transparency in our verification work.

**Evidence (Collect Evidence):** After contacting the claimant, HaqCheck gathers all relevant evidence to verify the claim. We prioritize primary sources, such as official documents, data, or direct statements, for their reliability and accuracy. When primary sources are unavailable, we carefully select credible secondary sources and document the reasoning behind their use. This evidence is thoroughly reviewed and cross-checked to ensure it supports a robust and accurate claim analysis.

**Consult (Consult Experts):** Once we've secured the evidence and broader context, we discuss it with specialist experts who can help us understand the data and provide analysis. Their expertise ensures our analysis is accurate, comprehensive, and informed by the best available knowledge. HaqCheck only quotes experts willing to go on the record, as we do not use anonymous sources. This step is particularly valuable for technical or complex topics, where expert input strengthens the credibility and depth of our fact-checks.

**Knowledge (Draft and Review):** After gathering evidence and consulting experts, HaqCheck drafts the fact-check report. This step involves clearly presenting the claim, the context, the evidence collected, and the analysis that supports the conclusion. The Lead Fact-checker then reviews the draft, ensuring that it meets our accuracy, clarity, and impartiality standards. This thorough review process is essential for refining the fact-check before it moves to the editing stage.

**Edit (Edit and Publish):** After the draft has been reviewed, it is handed over to the Editors for final editing. The editors refine the language, verify source attributions, and ensure the content aligns with HaqCheck's editorial standards. Before publication, the Lead Fact-checker rechecks the story to confirm its accuracy and readiness. Once approved, the social media curator publishes the fact-check on HaqCheck's website and shares it on social media platforms, ensuring broad reach and engagement.

**Develop (Feedback and Impact):** After publication, HaqCheck actively monitors audience feedback and evaluates the impact of fact-checking. Feedback is collected through comments, messages, and other engagement channels, helping us identify areas for improvement and address any concerns. We use this input to refine our processes and ensure that our fact-checks meet the highest accuracy and relevance standards. Additionally, we assess the broader impact of our work in fostering informed discourse and combating misinformation.

## How We Rate Our Fact Checks

After the fact-checkers are done with their research and get the best publicly available evidence, they will decide the rating the claim should have. The research editor will then cross-check the rating. If they don't all agree, they will discuss it in a team and take a vote.

Below, We Explain the Meaning of the Ratings We Use:

- **False:** The claim is inaccurate according to the best publicly available evidence. This includes conspiracy theories, false context of an image or video, Fabricated content
- **True:** The claim is correct and accurate according to the best publicly available evidence.
- **Misleading:** There might be elements that are accurate but presented in a misleading way. This includes False connections.
- **Satire:** The claim is satirical and intended to be taken as a joke
- **Altered:** The content has been manipulated, faked, or transformed audio, video, or photos.
- **Partly False:** The content has some factual inaccuracies. A mix of both True and False claims.
- **Scam:** When the claim is a dishonest scheme, or fraud, and uses a trick to deceive. They often have "too good to be true" offers and also impersonate a genuine source.

## Identifying and Citing Sources

HaqCheck lists all important sources of evidence used in its fact-checks and provides links to those sources whenever possible, allowing users to verify the accuracy of its work. If identifying a source would put the source's safety at risk, HaqCheck will provide as much detail about the source as possible without compromising their safety.

## Users Can Send Us Claims

HaqCheck encourages social media users to send us claims to check. We decide which claims to fact-check based on our policy and guidelines.

## Non-partisan Policy

HaqCheck remains committed to nonpartisanship. Our staff, independent from political affiliations or movements, strictly avoids advocacy or publishing policy opinions that might influence the perceived impartiality of our fact checks. To enhance transparency, we disclose the relevant interests of sources and any potential commercial relationships that could impact the accuracy of evidence. Our website clearly outlines this policy, ensuring readers understand its application. Our team receives regular training on these standards, and compliance is monitored through systematic audits to maintain our commitment to unbiased fact-checking.

## Correction Policy

At HaqCheck, transparency and accountability are paramount. Sometimes, mistakes happen, and while some are weaknesses, some are inevitable. We are open to correcting them promptly in whatever material we publish on our platforms. Our policy differentiates between "Corrections" for factual inaccuracies and "Updates" for adding new information or clarifications to existing content.

Corrections involve swiftly and transparently rectifying factual mistakes, clearly explaining to our readers what was incorrect and how it has been addressed.

Updates include new information that changes the context or enhances the clarity of a report. These are labeled distinctly and explain the new data and how it impacts the understanding of the subject.

We ensure all changes are noted directly in the content, maintaining the integrity of our fact-checking process. For substantial updates or ethical concerns, an editor's note is added to detail the nature of the changes.

Feedback is vital for continual improvement, and we encourage our audience to engage with us. We are committed to addressing all feedback and ensuring our content remains accurate and reflective of the latest information.

This policy underscores our dedication to delivering reliable and trustworthy information to our audience.
